Questions

What actually is Mira-cle?

A GitHub repository run as a workroom. The public side is this page. The members' side is the workroom, its Discussions, and its issue queue — where the work is done in view of the people funding it.

Why $2?

Because it should be an easy yes. The room doesn't need many people to work; it needs people who actually want to be in it. A low number keeps the door wide and the room honest.

Do I need a GitHub account?

To read the workroom, no. To join Discussions or open a request in the queue, yes — a free GitHub account is enough.

How do I cancel?

Your Stripe receipt email has a link to manage the subscription. Cancel there and it stops at the end of the billing period. No message to Mira required.

Is the code here free to use?

The site and tooling in this repository are MIT-licensed — take them. The writing, drafts, and members' material are Mira's and are not licensed for redistribution.

What if I pay and the room is empty?

Then cancel — genuinely. It's two dollars and you owe no explanation. The room is worth what's in it, and that's on Mira to keep true.

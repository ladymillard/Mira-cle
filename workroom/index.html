02 — Ownership

Everything built for
you is yours.

  • Outright. No percentage, no revenue share, no licence back to Mira. Sell it, rename it, take it elsewhere, keep 100% of what it earns.
  • Permanently. The $2 is the whole of Mira's compensation. Cancel and what's already been built for you stays built.
  • In public. That's the trade that keeps it $2 — your job becomes something the next member can fork. Don't bring commercially sensitive material into the room.
03 — Craft

How to bring
a good job

  • Say what you need to be able to do. "People should book me without texting me" beats "build me a React calendar component." The outcome is yours to specify; the method is Mira's problem.
  • Say why it matters. The context is usually what makes a result good rather than merely correct.
  • Check the shelf first. Forking something takes twenty minutes. Building from nothing takes days.
  • Back other people's jobs. 👍 is the priority list. Backing a job close to yours gets you both there faster.

House rules

  • The link stays in the room. There's no lock on the door. Passing the URL around is the only thing that actually breaks this.
  • Rough work is welcome work. Nothing on the bench is defended as finished. Critique the thinking, not the polish.
  • Say when it missed. A job built wrong and quietly abandoned helps nobody. Say so and it gets another pass.
  • Leave when it stops being worth it. Cancelling is a neutral act. A room people stay in out of guilt is a worse room.

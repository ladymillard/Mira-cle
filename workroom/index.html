02 — Ownership

What you make
here is yours.

  • Outright. No percentage, no revenue share, no licence back to Mira. Sell it, rename it, ship it under your own name, keep everything it earns.
  • Permanently. The $2 is the whole of Mira's compensation. Cancel whenever you like and what you built stays yours.
  • In public. That's the trade that keeps it $2 — what you find, the room gets. Don't bring commercially sensitive material onto the floor.
03 — Craft

How to push well

  • Go at the thing that annoys you. The itch is better information than a roadmap. If something here feels wrong to use, that's the push.
  • Build it rough first. Find the edge fast, then decide whether it's worth doing properly. Mira writes quicker than you can review — spend your attention on judgement, not typing.
  • Write down where it broke. A precisely described limit is worth more to the room than a half-finished feature. Both belong on the shelf.
  • Check the shelf first. Someone may have already hit this wall and mapped it.

House rules

  • The link stays in the room. There's no lock on the door. Passing the URL around is the only thing that actually breaks this.
  • Rough work is welcome work. Nothing on the bench is defended as finished. Critique the thinking, not the polish.
  • Say when it missed. Something built wrong and quietly abandoned helps nobody. Say so and it gets another pass.
  • Leave when it stops being worth it. Cancelling is a neutral act. A room people stay in out of guilt is a worse room.

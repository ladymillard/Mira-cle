House rules

  1. The link stays in the room

    No lock on the door. Passing the URL around is the only way to actually break this.

  2. Rough work is welcome work

    Nothing in here is defended as finished. Critique the thinking, not the polish.

  3. Ask for the real thing

    Vague requests get vague work. Say what you actually want and why it matters to you.

  4. Leave when it stops being worth it

    Cancelling is a neutral act. A room people stay in out of guilt is a worse room.
